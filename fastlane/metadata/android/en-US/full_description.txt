How are your times tables doing?

Choose the multiplication tables you want to practise, from 2 to 9, then slash through the digits of your answer. For 56, swipe from 5 to 6.

Don’t know the answer? It appears so you can try again. The ones you haven’t learned yet will come back more often.

SlashFacts is free and works offline, with no ads or account to create. No data is collected. Your progress stays on your device.
