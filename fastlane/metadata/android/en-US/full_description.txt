<p>SlashFacts is a multiplication-tables trainer (2 to 9) built around a single gesture: you answer by drawing one continuous stroke through the digits of the result. No buttons, no rewards, no screens between attempts — <b>see → remember → slash → next</b>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Pick the tables to practise, and each round adapts to the facts you keep missing or answer slowly.</li>
<li>A missed fact shows its answer and comes straight back until it is answered right.</li>
<li>The Facts map ticks every fact answered right first time — one mark a child can read at a glance.</li>
<li>Haptic feedback on every answer, fully usable offline.</li>
</ul>
<p>Designed for children: no account, no network access, no analytics, no ads. Everything stays on the device.</p>
