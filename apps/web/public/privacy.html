SlashFacts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

SlashFacts is a multiplication-tables practice app for iOS, Android, and the web. This page describes, in full, what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

SlashFacts collects nothing. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no network connection of any kind once installed. It works fully offline.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps a record of which multiplication facts you have practised — whether each was answered correctly, how long it took, and how many you have mastered — so it can adapt future rounds and show your own progress back to you. This record:

  • is stored only on your device (in the app's local database);
  • is never transmitted anywhere — not to us, not to any third party;
  • is never linked to your name, email, or any other identity, because the app never asks for one;
  • can be permanently erased at any time from the app's FACTS screen (RESET).

Third parties

SlashFacts does not integrate any third-party SDK: no analytics, no advertising network, no social login, no crash reporter. Nothing you do in the app is shared with anyone.

Children

SlashFacts is designed to be used by children practising multiplication tables. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, there is nothing for a parent to review, correct, or have deleted beyond using RESET on the device itself.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud-sync feature — this page will be updated first, and the change will be described plainly, in the same terms as above.
